# Your privacy choices — Tank Talks

Source: https://tanktalks.sdfkjh.com/legal/privacy

If you just want the short version: we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes, we don't run third-party ad networks, and we don't have user accounts. The most personal data we hold about any individual reader is "subscribed to our newsletter" or "clicked an affiliate link" — and you can opt out of both at any time.

## What we collect

Tank Talks collects a small amount of information from readers, depending on what you do on the site:

**Standard server logs.** Like virtually every website, the servers that host Tank Talks log basic information about each visit: your IP address, browser type, the page you visited, the time, and the page that referred you. This is needed for the site to work and to defend against attacks. These logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to build a profile of you.

**Newsletter subscription.** If you sign up for our weekly email, we collect your email address and the date you subscribed. That's it. We do not enrich this with third-party data and do not sell or rent the list.

**Affiliate clicks.** When you click an affiliate link on our site, the destination retailer (typically Amazon) records that the click came from us. We don't see who you are; we just see that a click happened. This is how affiliate commissions work across the entire web.

We do not collect:

-   Your name or address (unless you provide them in an email to us)
-   Payment information (we don't process payments directly)
-   Browsing behavior across other websites
-   Any data via tracking pixels or fingerprinting

## What we do NOT do

To be explicit about things we don't do:

-   We do not run third-party advertising networks (no Google AdSense, no display ads).
-   We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone for marketing purposes.
-   We do not use cross-site tracking technology.
-   We do not have user accounts or stored profiles.

The "share" word matters under California law. Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, "sharing" specifically includes disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't do that.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Tank Talks uses minimal cookies:

-   A small cookie may be set by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for security purposes (DDoS protection, bot detection). This is functional and not used for advertising.
-   If you sign up for the newsletter, our email service provider may set a cookie to verify the signup.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If you'd prefer not to allow even functional cookies, your browser settings should give you control.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have specific legal rights regarding your personal information.

### California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the following rights:

-   **Right to know** what personal information we collect, use, disclose, or sell about you.
-   **Right to delete** the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
-   **Right to correct** inaccurate personal information.
-   **Right to opt out of sale or sharing** of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
-   **Right to limit use of sensitive personal information** (we don't collect any, but you have the right anyway).
-   **Right to non-discrimination** — we cannot deny service or charge you differently because you exercise these rights.

**As stated above, Tank Talks does not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes.** There is therefore nothing for you to opt out of in that specific category. You may still exercise your other rights at any time.

### EU and UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your personal data. You may also object to processing and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We rely on legitimate interest for analytics, on consent for the newsletter, and on contract performance for serving the site.

### Everyone else

We extend the substantive rights above to all readers regardless of location, on a best-effort basis, even where not legally required.

## How to exercise your rights

To exercise any right described above, email **hello@tanktalks.sdfkjh.com** with the subject line "Privacy request" and tell us what you'd like us to do. We will respond within 30 days.

For verification, we may ask you to confirm the request from the email address associated with your activity on the site (typically your newsletter subscription). If you have not given us any personal information, there is generally nothing for us to delete or disclose.

You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. If you do, we may require proof that you authorized the agent.

## Children's privacy

Tank Talks is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

## Newsletter unsubscribe

Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. Clicking it removes you from the list immediately and permanently. If you never received our emails or never subscribed, no action is needed.

If you have trouble unsubscribing, email **hello@tanktalks.sdfkjh.com** and we will remove you manually.

## Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected:

-   Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe.
-   Server logs: typically 30–90 days, then anonymized or deleted.
-   Email correspondence: until the matter is resolved, then archived for a reasonable period in case you follow up.

## Changes to this policy

When we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify newsletter subscribers by email.
