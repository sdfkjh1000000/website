# Tank Talks

Tank Talks is a **freshwater aquarium editorial site** covering fish, shrimp, aquatic
plants, and water chemistry. It pairs practical how-to and troubleshooting articles with
disclosed Amazon affiliate gear roundups. The focus is freshwater only — community tanks,
nano tanks, planted tanks, and shrimp keeping.

## Topics covered

The nitrogen cycle, fishless cycling, nitrate control, dwarf shrimp keeping (Neocaridina
and Caridina), shrimp acclimation and breeding, aquatic mosses and plants (Java moss,
Christmas moss, Bucephalandra, marimo), and nano fish stocking for small planted tanks.
